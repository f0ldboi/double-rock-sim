How to play

Double rock

  1. Stand still. Doom throws two rocks, one after the other.
  2. When rock 1 flashes white, click a tile a few steps away. Its boulder lands where you were standing.
  3. Stay off the dark shadows. Rocks fall on them.
  4. When rock 2 flashes white, step onto a tile where rock 1's rocks just fell. Rock 2's never fall there.
  5. When rock 2 bursts, turn on Protect from Magic for a blue rock, or Protect from Missiles for a green one.
  6. Switch prayers for each of the six hits. Blue rock: Magic, Missiles, Magic, Magic, Missiles, Magic. Green rock: the same with the two swapped.

Rockblock + punish

  1. You start on green tile 1, below Doom.
  2. When rock 1 flashes white, click green tile 2.
  3. When rock 2 flashes white, click the special attack orb, then click Doom.
  4. You run up and hit Doom with the halberd. That stops the yellow bar over its head before its beam fires.
  5. Pray the six hits, the same as in Double rock.
